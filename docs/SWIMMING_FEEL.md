# Swimming Feel

Swimming is a distinct movement state rather than ordinary walking on a water tile.

- Flippers are required before water becomes traversable.
- Swimming preserves immediate directional control but moves at roughly 72% of normal land speed.
- Dash is disabled while swimming so water remains a meaningful terrain state.
- The player receives a subtle bob, waterline and ripple while swimming.
- Entering/leaving water stays continuous; the game does not take control away for a canned transition.
- Existing collision, room, save and equipment systems remain unchanged.

The intent is readable classic top-down adventure movement: water should feel different immediately without becoming slow or frustrating.
