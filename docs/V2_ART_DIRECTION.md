# V2 Visual Direction

Legend of Developers uses an original 16-bit-inspired top-down adventure art direction built around a restrained fantasy palette rather than the earlier neon/cyber presentation.

## Core palette

- Slate/navy: structure, hero clothing, UI framing
- Warm ochre/gold: important affordances, equipment accents, treasure and magic highlights
- Earth brown: wood, leather, village architecture and handles
- Muted forest green: vegetation and nature magic
- Silver/stone: blades, dungeon architecture and mechanical objects
- Warm red/clay: damage, pottery, fire and danger

## Rendering rules

- Prefer V2 catalog art before legacy assets.
- Keep nearest-neighbor/pixel-snapped rendering.
- Use strong readable silhouettes at gameplay scale.
- Avoid electric cyan/magenta unless a future location deliberately establishes them as a localized biome identity.
- Interactive objects must look consistent while idle, carried and thrown.
- Combat effects should reinforce the V2 palette rather than visually reverting to the old cyber theme.

## Originality

V2 artwork committed under `react-vite/public/art/v2` is original repository-owned artwork. External permissive/CC0 packs may be introduced only when their source and license are documented and their style is intentionally integrated rather than mixed randomly with the existing game.
