# Gameplay Audio

Legend of Developers uses a lightweight synthesized Web Audio SFX layer for immediate gameplay feedback without depending on licensed sound packs.

Initial cues cover sword swings, confirmed hits, merchant pickups, chest openings, ledge hops, pit falls, thrown objects, water entry/exit, and room/map transitions. Audio is unlocked on the first keyboard input to satisfy browser autoplay policies.

The cues are intentionally short and restrained. They reinforce timing and impact rather than becoming constant background noise. Music remains a separate future concern so combat feedback can be tuned independently.
